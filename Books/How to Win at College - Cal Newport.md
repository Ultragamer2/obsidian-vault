# How to Win at College - Cal Newport

Source: Book by Cal Newport, 2005
Date read: 2026-09-16 kickoff
Study Hacks link: https://calnewport.com/getting-things-done-for-college-students-introduction/

## Capture
- Do not use daily todo list, use calendar + time blocks
- Schedule free time, protect deep study blocks
- Empty inbox, capture fast

## QEC Notes
- Q: How to manage time without burnout in college?
- E: Interviews with Rhodes, Marshall, Truman winners plus 50 straight-A students. Common habit was morning planning + calendar, not longer hours.
- C: Simple trusted system beats heroic effort.

- Q: How to break up big work?
- E: Early Study Hacks series promises followups on breaking and scheduling work. Book rules 12, 23, 65, 71 point to batching and placement.
- C: Start with capture + morning plan, then add work-breakdown next.

- Q: How to prioritize what matters?
- E: Activity filter idea from Monday Master Class series in 2007.
- C: Treat high value deep work first, push extras to future dates.

## 3 Big Ideas
1. Calendar over list. Lists grow, calendars force tradeoffs.
2. Capture immediately. Scrap paper or phone inbox, then morning move to calendar.
3. Audit time. Map when and where you will work to see real capacity.
