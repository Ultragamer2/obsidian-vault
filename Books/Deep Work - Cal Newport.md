## Capture
- Deep work: professional activities in a distraction-free state that push cognitive limits
- Shallow work: non-cognitively demanding, often performed distracted
- 4-hour deep work cap is realistic; past that, returns drop sharply
- Track deep hours weekly as a scorecard metric

## QEC Notes
- Q: Why is deep work rare and valuable in modern work?
- E: Knowledge economy rewards rapid skill acquisition and high-quality output; both require sustained focus, which open offices and notifications destroy.
- C: Deep work is not a habit but a craft you train with rituals, scheduling, and environment design; willpower is not enough.

## 3 Big Ideas
1. Deep work is like a skill sport — you need rituals (same place, time, rules) and a shutdown routine so your brain releases the problem after the session.
2. The metric is depth, not busyness: 2 focused hours producing a working model beats 8 distracted hours of email and half-reading.
3. Embrace boredom; constant stimulation trains the brain to crave distraction. Letting your mind wander strengthens the focus muscle for deep sessions.
