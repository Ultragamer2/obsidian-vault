## Capture
- Passion is not found, it's cultivated through skill.
- Build career capital (rare + valuable skills) before demanding autonomy.
- Craftsperson mindset > passion mindset.

## QEC Notes
- Q: Does following passion work?
- E: Newport shows passion-chasers often end up lost; skill-builders gain autonomy.
- C: Focus on what you offer, not what the world owes you.

## 3 Big Ideas
1. "Follow your passion" is bad advice — passion grows from mastery.
2. Career capital = rare skills traded for control and meaning.
3. Craftsman mindset: relentlessly improve craft (like Steve Martin).
