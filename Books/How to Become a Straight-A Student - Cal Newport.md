## QEC Notes
- Q: Why do some students with less raw effort score higher?
- E: Newport interviews top students; common factor is structured scheduling + active recall, not longer hours.
- C: Straight-A is a system problem, not intelligence; design the system first.

## 3 Big Ideas
1. Pseudo-work is disguised procrastination — long hours of unfocused work feel productive but produce nothing. Measure output, not time.
2. A weekly calendar plus daily to-do list beats pure willpower; planning removes decision fatigue before studying.
3. Active recall (self-testing) dominates passive re-reading; the brain remembers retrieval, not exposure.
