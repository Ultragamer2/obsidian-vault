## QEC Notes
- Q: How to stay steady with classes plus work?
- E: Book 2: Begin the morning by saying to thyself, I shall meet with the busybody, the ungrateful, arrogant. None of this surprises the prepared mind. Book 4: order time, do not be carried along.
- C: Morning preview plus evening review beats motivation.

- Q: How to handle criticism and hype threads?
- E: Book 8: If it is not right do not do it, if it is not true do not say it. Book 6: attend to the matter before thee.
- C: Capture noise to inbox, judge once on calendar, not all day.

- Q: How to plan large projects without dread?
- E: Book 7: Do not look at the whole mass, take each part as it comes. Matches Cal three steps for large projects.
- C: Break to next physical block, place on calendar.

## 5 Big Ideas for students
1. Preview and review. 5 minutes morning, 5 minutes night. Same as Cal morning plan.
2. Inbox the judgments. Write the irritation down, decide later.
3. One thing at a time. Do the matter before thee with full attention.
4. Obstacle training. Missed shift or bug becomes reps.
5. Finite ledger. Count days, schedule deep first.
