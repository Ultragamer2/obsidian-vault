Title: Capture First, Then Plan - A UCF Take

Structure I am copying from him: series intro, case foundation, system gap.

1. What he was doing in 2007
He opened a time management series for students. He had interviewed business consultants, then Rhodes and Marshall winners, then 50 straight-A students. Pattern he kept seeing was not longer hours, it was external capture plus short morning planning.

2. Case foundation
Think of two students. One keeps everything in head and runs on a long todo list. Other carries one inbox card and spends 5 minutes each morning placing tasks on a calendar with a when and where. Second student feels less stress because the system holds the tasks, and sees real capacity because the day is mapped.

3. System for you
Capture: one inbox, paper or phone, everything goes there immediately.
Morning: move deadlines to dates, assign to-dos to days, build today as time blocks.
Audit: if today overflows, push extras forward instead of pretending.

Gap he flagged: starter system does not break down big projects or rank by importance. That is next in his series and next in our vault.
