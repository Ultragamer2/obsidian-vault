# Synthesis 003 — Talkers vs Doers

**Source:** https://calnewport.com/what-separates-talkers-and-doers/ (2007 essay)
**Emulation, not by Cal.** No logo, no claim of authorship.

## TL;DR
Doers differ by internal commitment, "good enough" standards, short-term next steps (2-4 weeks), sharing intentions for accountability, and tracking real vs projected results. Fear kills action more than planning does.

## 1. Action Habit
Doers keep promises to themselves. Motivation is internal, not external approval.

## 2. Good Enough
Perfection delays start. Set a usable standard and move.

## 3. Short-Term Future
At meeting end: ask "what are next steps?" Chunk vision into 2-4 week actions, not distant dreams.

## 4. Accountability
Share intentions. Public commitment creates external pressure.

## 5. Track Results
Compare actual vs projected at least quarterly; write why.

## Action Box
This week: pick one pending idea, define the 2-week next step, share it with one person, and schedule it.
